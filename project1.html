Theory

Probability theory studies random events and the possibility of their occurrence.

In information theory, probability is used to describe the uncertainty of an event and to calculate the amount of information.

Combinatorics studies different ways of selecting and arranging objects. Factorials, permutations, combinations, and arrangements are basic combinatorial concepts.

1. Factorial

The factorial of a non-negative integer n is the product of all positive integers from 1 to n.

n! = 1 · 2 · 3 · ... · n

Factorial Calculator

2. Permutations

Permutations describe the number of possible orders of n different objects.

P(n) = n!

Permutations Calculator

3. Combinations

Combinations determine the number of ways to choose k objects from n objects without considering their order.

C(n,k) = n! / (k!(n-k)!)

Combinations Calculator

4. Permutations with Repetition

Permutations with repetition are used when some objects are identical.

P = n! / k!

Permutations with Repetition Calculator

5. Arrangements

Arrangements are used to select and order k objects from n objects.

A(n,k) = n! / (n-k)!
